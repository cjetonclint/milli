Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

How early should I book my date?

Popular wedding dates can book many months in advance, so reaching out as soon as your date and venue are confirmed is recommended.

Do you travel for weddings?

Yes. Toronto, the GTA and destination weddings can be accommodated depending on availability and travel requirements.

Do you offer a trial session?

Yes. Bridal previews can be arranged to refine the makeup and hair direction before the wedding day.

How long does bridal makeup take?

Timing depends on the selected services and look. A custom wedding-day schedule is created once the booking details are confirmed.

Do you provide saree draping?

Yes. Saree draping can be added to selected bridal and event services.

Can you accommodate bridal parties?

Yes. Include the number of people and requested services in your inquiry so timing and staffing can be planned properly.

What is your cancellation policy?

Final booking terms, retainers, changes and cancellation policies are provided with your service agreement.
